# Stratified Signal

Stratified Signal treats complex systems as layered fields of intent, movement, and evidence. Its visual language is built from precise strata: broad horizontal bands for domains of responsibility, narrow connective lines for controlled transitions, and compact nodes that feel engineered rather than decorative. Space is used as the primary explanation; distance implies boundary, alignment implies dependency, and vertical progression implies transformation.

Color is restrained and functional, with deep ink backgrounds, muted slate surfaces, and selective luminous accents. Each accent must carry meaning rather than decoration: blue for orchestration, green for retrieval and evidence, amber for decision and evaluation, violet for memory and context. The final work should feel meticulously crafted, as if every contrast level and edge radius was calibrated through painstaking attention.

Scale communicates authority. Large architectural bands hold the eye, while small labels sit like instrument markings on a technical artifact. Text remains sparse, clinical, and essential-only. The design should never explain through paragraphs; it should allow relationships to become visible through composition. The result must look like the product of deep expertise, not a slide assembled quickly.

Rhythm emerges from repetition: repeated node geometry, repeated connector spacing, repeated reference markers, repeated micro-labels. These repetitions should feel deliberate and master-level, like a system diagram produced by someone at the top of their field after countless hours of refinement. The craft lies in the quiet consistency of alignment, proportion, and visual restraint.

Composition balances analytic clarity with museum-grade calm. The artifact should feel like a scientific map of an invisible workflow: observable, inspectable, but still elegant. Nothing should appear accidental. Every boundary, line, label, and empty space must be labored over with care, producing a polished diagram where engineering architecture becomes visual form.
